===============================================
INVOICE & QUOTATION GENERATOR - QUICK USER TEST
===============================================

This is a simple 4-step test to verify the app is working correctly.

STEP 1: Add a Client
---------------------
1. Open the app (index.html or https://todo-robin.vercel.app)
2. Click "Clients" tab
3. Click "+ Add Client"
4. Fill in:
   - Name: "John Smith"
   - Email: "john@example.com"
   - Address: "123 Main St"
   - Phone: "555-1234"
5. Click "Save"
✓ Verify: Client appears in the clients list

STEP 2: Add a Product
---------------------
1. Click "Products" tab
2. Click "+ Add Product"
3. Fill in:
   - Name: "Web Design Service"
   - Unit Price: "500"
   - Description: "Professional website design"
4. Click "Save"
✓ Verify: Product appears in the products list

STEP 3: Create a Quotation & Convert to Invoice
------------------------------------------------
1. Click "Quotations" tab
2. Click "+ New Quotation"
3. Fill in:
   - Client: Select "John Smith"
   - Tax Rate: "10"
4. Click "+ Add Line Item"
5. Fill in:
   - Product: Select "Web Design Service"
   - Qty: "2"
   - Price: Auto-fills to "500"
6. Verify totals update: Subtotal = 1000, Tax = 100, Total = 1100
7. Click "Save Quotation"
8. Click "Convert to Invoice" on the quotation
✓ Verify: Invoice appears with auto-generated number (INV-2026-001)

STEP 4: Download Invoice & Test Status
---------------------------------------
1. Click "Invoices" tab
2. Click "View" on the invoice
3. In preview, click "Download PDF"
✓ Verify: PDF file downloads with name "INV-2026-001.pdf"
4. Close preview and click "Status"
5. Change status to "Sent" and click "Save"
✓ Verify: Status badge updates to "Sent"

===============================================
ALL TESTS PASSED? App is working correctly! ✓
===============================================

Notes:
- All data is saved to browser storage (persists after page reload)
- Toggle theme (🌙/☀️) to test dark mode
- Check "Ageing Report" tab to see unpaid invoices grouped by age
